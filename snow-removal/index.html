The work

What a snow visit covers

Snow removal is the service RWB started with and it is still what the phone rings for first. A visit tends to run in the same order. The plow clears the driving surface, walkways and steps get cleared where a plow cannot reach, and then salt or de-icer goes down so the surface does not glaze back over once the temperature drops after dark.

Storms that run long get more than one pass. Clearing a parking lot once at midnight does not help much if another four inches lands before the morning, so repeat visits are planned into the way a storm gets worked rather than treated as a second job.

RWB provides snow removal as part of a year-round service operation across New York City and Long Island. The company is open 24 hours and provides 24-hour emergency services, so a storm that starts at two in the morning is not a problem.

  • Driveways, parking lots and private roads
  • Walkways, steps and building entrances
  • Salting and de-icing after the plowing
  • Repeat passes while a storm is still running

Property types

Residential and commercial snow removal

Both get plowed. The difference is mostly scale and what has to be open first thing in the morning.

Residential snow removal

Driveways, front walks, steps, side paths and the apron at the bottom of the driveway that the town plow fills straight back in. On a normal house the job is getting a car out and getting people to the front door without walking on ice.

  • Driveways and aprons
  • Front walks and steps
  • Side paths and gates

Commercial snow removal

Parking lots, loading areas, building entrances and private roads. Commercial work is usually about timing. A lot has to be open and safe before staff and customers arrive, which often means plowing overnight and coming back before the doors open.

  • Parking lots and loading areas
  • Building entrances and fire doors
  • Private roads and access lanes

Questions

Snow removal questions

Do you salt as well as plow?

Yes. Salting and de-icing are part of the same visit. Plowing moves the snow, and salt is what keeps the cleared surface from freezing into a sheet once the sun goes down.

Do you come back during a long storm?

Long storms get more than one pass. One visit at the start of a storm is rarely enough, so repeat passes are planned around how the storm is actually running rather than booked as separate jobs.

Do you plow parking lots and private roads?

Yes. Commercial parking lots, loading areas, apartment property and private roads are all part of the snow work, alongside residential driveways.

Are you available overnight and on holidays?

RWB is open 24 hours, every day, and provides 24-hour emergency services. Snow does not wait for business hours, so neither does the phone.

Do you clear walkways and steps by hand?

Where a plow cannot reach, yes. Walkways, steps and entrances get cleared so there is a safe path from the street to the door, not just a clear driveway.

Do you handle both houses and commercial property?

Both. Residential driveways and walkways, and commercial lots, entrances and access roads. Say which one you have when you call so the right setup shows up.
