The work

What we haul away

Junk removal is the part of the year that picks up as soon as the snow is gone. Old furniture, appliances, construction debris and the pile that has been quietly filling the garage since the last time anybody parked in it. RWB does the lifting and it goes on the truck.

You do not have to drag anything to the curb first. Most of the calls are about things that are heavy, awkward, or upstairs, which is usually why they are still sitting there. Sofas, mattresses, appliances and renovation debris all move the same way, by hand and onto the truck.

Junk hauling runs across New York City and Long Island for both houses and commercial property, and RWB is open 24 hours with 24-hour emergency services if the timing is tight.

  • Old furniture, sofas and mattresses
  • Appliances and other heavy single items
  • Construction and renovation debris
  • Garage, basement and yard clearouts

Questions

Junk removal questions

Do I have to carry anything out to the curb?

No. RWB does the lifting. The things people call about are usually heavy, awkward or upstairs, which is the whole reason they are still there.

Do you take construction and renovation debris?

Yes. Debris left behind after work finishes is one of the more common hauling calls, on both houses and commercial property.

Do you take appliances and furniture?

Yes. Sofas, mattresses, tables, refrigerators, washers and similar heavy single items are standard junk removal work.

Do you do commercial cleanouts?

Yes. Units being turned over, offices being emptied and commercial space that has to be clear by a date are all part of the hauling work.

Is junk removal available in winter too?

Yes. Hauling picks up once the snow is gone, but RWB runs all year and is open 24 hours with 24-hour emergency services.
